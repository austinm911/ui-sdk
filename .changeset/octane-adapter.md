Adds `@ui-sdk/octane`, UI SDK for Octane, written as Strong-mode `.tsrx` with the same components and parts as `@ui-sdk/react`.

In core, a drawer backdrop that renders after its drawer opened now fades in with it, a tree item that becomes a branch after it rendered now expands with Alt+Right and expand-all, and a popup in a hidden page now takes its sheet's viewport out of the top layer.
